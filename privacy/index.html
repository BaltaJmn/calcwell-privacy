Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Calcwell does not collect your content, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything you make stays on your iPhone. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

Your calculations

The numbers you type, the stack, the memories, the worksheets and the tape are saved on your iPhone so they are still there the next time you open Calcwell. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. You can clear the tape and the worksheets in the app, and everything is removed if you delete the app.

Copying and exporting

When you copy the tape or export it, or an amortization schedule, as text or CSV, you choose where it goes (the clipboard, Files, Mail, another app...). Calcwell does not send it anywhere by itself.

Purchases

Calcwell Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Calcwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether Pro was bought with your Apple Account. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Calcwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Nothing else from Calcwell, such as your calculations, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Calcwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Calcwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, described under Purchases. Calcwell needs no permissions and works with the network turned off; the purchase record is sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Calcwell is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID and the purchase record (see Purchases).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: baltajmn@gmail.com. The developer is Baltasar Jiménez Rincón, Spain (details in the legal notice).
