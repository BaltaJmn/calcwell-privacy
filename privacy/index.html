Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Calcwell does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Your calculations

The numbers you type, the stack, the memories, the worksheets and the tape are saved on your iPhone so they are still there the next time you open Calcwell. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. You can clear the tape and the worksheets in the app, and everything is removed if you delete the app.

Copying and exporting

When you copy the tape or export it, or an amortization schedule, as text or CSV, you choose where it goes (the clipboard, Files, Mail, another app...). Calcwell does not send it anywhere by itself.

Purchases

Calcwell Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Calcwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it only asks Apple whether Pro was bought with your Apple Account. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Calcwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data. Calcwell needs no permissions and works with the network turned off.

Children

Calcwell is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact
