Calcwell

A financial calculator you can trust to the cent

Calcwell is a financial calculator for iPhone, in RPN with a four-level stack or in algebraic mode. Solve any of N, I/YR, PV, PMT and FV for loans, mortgages, savings and leases, then go further with amortization, cash flows, bonds, dates and statistics.

No account, no server of ours. Everything is calculated and saved on your iPhone. Calcwell has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking, and it works with the network turned off. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID (see the privacy policy).

What it does

Time value of money with payments at the beginning or end of the period, any number of payments and compounding periods a year. Amortization schedules rounded to the cent, with the last payment adjusted so the loan closes at zero. Net present value, every internal rate of return when the cash flows change sign more than once, MIRR and payback. Bond price and yield with accrued interest and duration, days between dates (actual and 30/360), mean, standard deviation and linear regression, straight-line, sum-of-years and declining-balance depreciation, and nominal to effective rates.

When something can't be solved, Calcwell says why in a plain sentence on the display, and the stack stays as it was. A tape keeps every calculation, so you can check your work.

Checked against known answers

Every release is checked against more than 1,800 reference cases worked out separately, among them textbook and spreadsheet examples and 1,460 mortgages, and must match all of them to the cent.
